# Coverage and source policy

Scope: all 187 Lycian, 264 Carian and two Milyan catalogue entries visible
in the selected public eDiAna index captured across 2026-10-01/02 UTC.
All 32 public batch responses were obtained and membership reconciled.
A missing response, unexpected ID, duplicated ID, changed source title or
checksum mismatch fails the build.

One deleted Carian entry is a source catalogue slot, not an admitted Carian
inscription. The Coin Legends slot aggregates multiple legends; neither
its entry count nor its individual rows establish a physical witness count.
Fragments, joins and catalogue labels remain upstream designations. Object
identity is null pending a separately audited concordance. TL 44 occurs in
Lycian and Milyan source partitions; this does not create two independent
monuments, nor does it justify merging their language components.

No TITUS restricted corpus or unlicensed modern printed edition was copied.
Their editions can support later comparisons if reuse terms permit. New
sources must carry their own licence/rights evidence, attribution, version,
checksums, a documented adapter and an ID/object reconciliation report.

Next gates: update the archival Lycian source against newer licensed
editions; audit physical-object and reading-version concordances; verify
Greek parallel assignments and source tokenization; obtain independent
expert review. Keep native glyph occurrences distinct from transliteration
strings. Preserve disagreements as attributed alternatives.
