# Linear A method parity and rights frontier

The 453-entry eDiAna snapshot is a strong, reproducible digital source corpus: 187 Lycian, 264 Carian and 2 Milyan catalogue entries. It is not asserted to be a unique physical-monument count or an independently collated critical edition.

Lycian lineage runs substantially through Kalinka/Friedrich/Neumann to Melchert and eDiAna; TITUS explicitly describes its re-editing and warns that readings should be checked. Carian numbering/transliteration in eDiAna is based on Adiego 2007 with later publications added. Milyan remains a separate partition.

Method-parity controls now make edition dependence, uncertainty/direction disagreements, partition boundaries, component rights, browser/API/exports and validation explicit.

Further material advancement requires rights-compatible systematic access to modern Lycian critical evidence and Adiego's Carian critical corpus, plus object-level catalogue evidence sufficient to reconcile digital entries to physical monuments. Do not reconstruct protected editions by combining dependent digital copies. Expert epigraphic/object/linguistic adjudication remains downstream.
